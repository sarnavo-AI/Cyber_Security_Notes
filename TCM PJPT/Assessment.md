
# Different Types of Assessment

## External Network Pentest

It's when you assess the attack surface as an outsider.

- Heavily relies on OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) Gathering
	- To get see the breaches on that organization in past
	- To see any hacked password / format of email
	
- Typical lasts 32-40 hours(in small networks), then 8-16 hours for report writing
		
- Since the org already have patched everything, very tough to find a RCEs, but we can know who are people working, Where are the login pages

## Internal Network Pentest

It's when you assess as an insider within the org's network.

- We have breached the perimeter
	- Usually done by sending a laptop to the client directly, (mostly REMOTE work)
	
- Main Method in internal Pen Test in **ACTIVE DIRECTORY** Attack
	- 99% of Fortune 500 company use active directory in their org
	
- Typical lasts 32-40 hours(in small networks), then 8-16 hours for report writing

## Web App Pentest

It's when you assess the Web Application Security.

	Very Important because there are possibly more web than networks
	
- Heavily relies on OWASP (Open Worldwide Application Security Project) Gathering
	- They give guidelines on:
		- How to Attack
		- How to Defend
		- How To Test
	
- Typical lasts 32-40 hours(in small networks), then 8-16 hours for report writing

## Wireless Pentest

It's when you assess the wireless network security of an org.

- Methodology depends on types of wireless device used:
	- WPA2-PSK
	- WPA2 Enterprise
	
- Typical lasts 4-8 hours(in small networks) per SSID, then 2-4 hours for report writing

## Physical Pentest and Social Engineering

It's when you assess org's physical security or end-user training.

- Methodology depends on goals:
	- Cloning Batches to enter the perimeter 
	- Getting into the server room without anyone noticing
	
- Another Aspect is the Social Engineering itself, that how ever the strong security protocols maybe but it's **HUMANs** who are the weakness
	
- Typical lasts 16-40 hours(in small orgs), then 4-8 hours for report writing
